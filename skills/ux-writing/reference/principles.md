# Product words must match product behavior

- Tell users what happened, what matters, and what they can actually do next.
- Prefer concrete actions and familiar domain language over promotional or technical filler.
- Keep recovery detail and consequences even when shortening a message.
- Use natural Korean consistent with the product's tone. Do not translate identifiers or break locale placeholders.
- Keep visible text and accessible names coherent, preserving meaningful labels.
- Treat error causes, success, time estimates, and undo as factual claims requiring product evidence.
- Reuse the shared [interaction contract](../../ui-craft-bundle/references/interaction-design.md) to understand what a message may promise.
